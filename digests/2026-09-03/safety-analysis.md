# Blogs & News (2026-09-03)

## Top Stories

**[Resolution launches new Agent Foundations team](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/qTNm8qzqhhpno58fZ/resolution-has-a-new-agent-foundations-team)** - A new research organization has formed to continue MIRI's agent foundations work, led by Jeremy Gillen and including former MIRI researchers Abram Demski, Sam Eisenstat, and Scott Garrabrant. The team aims to develop fundamental theory for understanding minds and plans to recruit additional experienced researchers plus junior staff. This represents a significant continuation of foundational AI alignment research that many consider crucial for long-term safety.

**[Kairos secures $50M for AI safety talent infrastructure](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/DRaePC8aqLYTbEjTD/kairos-has-raised-usd50m-to-build-talent-infrastructure-for)** - Kairos received one of the largest AI safety fieldbuilding commitments to date from Coefficient Giving, funding two years of operations to grow talent infrastructure and incubate new organizations. The organization has doubled in size over six months and plans to double again, with ten open roles across events, group support, and other functions. This injection of capital could significantly accelerate the pipeline of AI safety researchers and organizations.

**[Proposal for mass transcript monitoring organization](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/FbKbfXjHvmZd8wtrX/a-proposal-for-a-highly-effective-ai-safety-org)** - A LessWrong post proposes creating an organization that pays people to systematically review anonymized AI training transcripts flagged by high-recall monitors to catch warning shots and concerning behaviors. The author estimates $5M/month could fund 1000 reviewers to process all tokens in frontier RL runs and catch approximately 15 serious incidents monthly. This represents a novel approach to AI safety monitoring that could provide early detection of dangerous capabilities or misalignment.

**[Zvi analyzes Anthropic alignment challenges](https://thezvi.substack.com/p/anthropic-has-some-alignment-problems)** - AI safety commentator Zvi Mowshowitz published analysis suggesting Anthropic faces significant alignment problems, though the brief description provides limited detail about specific concerns. Given Anthropic's position as a leading safety-focused AI lab, any identified alignment issues could have broader implications for the field's approach to building safe AI systems.

**[AI-assisted cyberattacks target critical infrastructure](https://newsletter.safe.ai/p/aisn-80-ai-is-assisting-cyberattacks)** - The Center for AI Safety newsletter reports on AI being used to assist cyberattacks against critical infrastructure, with additional technical reports on the recent Hugging Face incident. This demonstrates the near-term dual-use risks of AI systems and highlights the need for robust security measures as AI capabilities continue advancing.
